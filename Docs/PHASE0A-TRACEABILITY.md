## Status and verification key

**Documented** means the amendment has a concrete design rule and future acceptance check, and the cited sections were inspected. It does not mean an app exists, a gate passed, or the user approved implementation. All application behavior evidence is pending; open risks and phase blockers are in [risk register](PHASE0A-RISKS.md). D1–D9 are document consistency inspections, recorded in [verification report](PHASE0A-CONSISTENCY.md#manual-consistency-review). D0 is the completed mechanical file/link/graph/coverage check in that report.

## Dependencies and remaining conditions

R1 governs all execution. R2 and R3 constrain the 0B package, target settings and G0 tests. R5 supplies the finite lifecycle tested by G0; its coordinator/cancellation behavior later depends on R4 grants and R8 storage. R3 workflow snapshots, workspace leases and revision hashes bind R4 approval and later GE verification. R6 provisioning and R7 aggregate memory limits must be measured together before simultaneous speech/inference is allowed. R8 sanitized metadata resolves privacy without discarding essential intent/outcome audit. GC/GE are distinct from G0: missing baseline OS proof blocks compatibility claims, while an unresolved execution-mode decision blocks general-purpose features. Neither permits automatic phase progression.

**Result:** 37 trace entries; R1–R8 and all five review gate rows mapped, plus three user-specific clarifications. The separate mode decision, actual compatibility/permission/resource/recovery tests and product thresholds remain open implementation evidence/feature gates, not unresolved amendment text. No mandatory architectural blocker to a separately authorized minimal 0B scope remains.
