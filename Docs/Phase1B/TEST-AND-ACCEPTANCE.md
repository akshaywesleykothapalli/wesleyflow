## Persisted invariants and failure handling

Wrong/currently moved root, stale snapshot, changed policy/descriptor, expired/revoked grant, forged public ID, unknown target or model-generated approval → no dispatch. Source/project/provider outputs are untrusted, never instructions to expand policy. Snapshot scopes do not authorize all files or executable configuration. File validation must independently read actual post-state via authorized handles; model text and broker claims alone do not prove verified completion.

Keep atomic state/event CAS, intent-before-effect, artifact/DB reconciliation, startup interruption and no restored grants. Storage stall/failure closes new effects immediately, preserves unknown acknowledgements and reports emergency uncommitted status. Partial edits are not atomic transactions; record applied/notApplied/uncertain per file, never auto-reset/replay/rollback user changes. Privacy/quota gates remain100MiB managed content,10MiB reserve and30-day eligible retention; active/uncertain evidence is not pruned. Raw source/prompt retention stays opt-in; no source contents in default diagnostics.

One inference lease/active task, FIFO16,20 inference turns/50 change-or-context requests/two explicitly authorized retries/15min family/5min operation limits remain ceilings. Progress may coalesce; approval/intent/outcome/evidence/terminal records cannot silently drop. Overflow/disconnect → fail dependent work and preserve an inspectable safe error. A bounded uncertain report is correct failure behavior, not a physical-stop pass; quarantine conflicting resources. Missing evidence keeps the corresponding feature disabled.

## Historical gate reconciliation and compatibility

Old B1 remains the completed fixture adapter. Broad B2 is **not passed** by 2A; it is replaced in the delivery plan by PB/PC/PE plus the distinct command subgate. Old B3–B5 are refined into PI/PE/PX without dropping resource, independent-verification or OS requirements. Historical reports are dated records; current readiness comes from this matrix and the adopted ADR, not historical recommendations to launch OpenCode.

Actual macOS26 runtime, full Xcode26.3/Swift6.2.3/macOS26.2 SDK compatibility, stable release signing/notarization, consent behavior and power-loss durability remain open. Xcode27beta/Swift6.4/SDK27 tests do not close them. Live inference, productive edits and general tool execution remain disabled until separately authorized and their applicable gates pass.
